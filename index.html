Instructions for Participants

This survey contains 14 questions divided into two categories:

  • Task 1: 7 questions (no time limit)
  • Task 2: 7 questions (time limit of 30 seconds each)

For Task 1, you have unlimited time to make decisions.

For Task 2, you must decide within 30 seconds. Once the time is up, the next question will appear automatically.

After each question, rate your confidence using this scale:

  1. Least Confidence
  2. Slightly Confident
  3. Moderately Confident
  4. Significantly Confident
  5. Fully Confident
